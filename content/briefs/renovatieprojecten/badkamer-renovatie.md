## Search intent / SERP format

Commercial-informational. The SERP mixes current cost guides, linear renovation steps, contractor pages and quote-generation pages. Users expect a useful cost orientation and enough planning detail to understand what a contractor must actually price.

Do **not** imitate the dominant generic `10 stappen + gemiddelde prijs` format. Match the intent while using a stronger decision model.

## Audience

Dutch homeowner who knows the bathroom needs work but has not yet fixed the final scope or signed an installation/renovation offer. Usually non-expert; may already be looking at sanitary ware, tiles or showroom proposals.

## Job to be done

Determine how invasive the bathroom renovation really is, settle the hidden technical interfaces before finishes/products are fixed, understand what current 2026 price benchmarks actually include, and prepare one bathroom scope that multiple contractors can quote consistently.

## Page ownership

This URL owns:
- bathroom-project scope;
- visible vs hidden work behind finishes;
- layout / plumbing / drainage / substrate-water-management / electrical / ventilation interfaces;
- current bathroom-specific benchmark with source/scope;
- bathroom-specific pre-offer readiness and quote items.

It does **not** own:
- whole-house renovation sequence → `/renovatie-plannen/renovatie-volgorde/`;

## Existing value to preserve

- `Eerst de scope, dan de tegels` principle.
- distinction between visible finish choices and technical choices behind them;
- early attention to ventilation, electricity and hidden moisture;
- conversion only after scope definition;
- independent tone and no fake urgency.

## MUST coverage

1. **Three levels of bathroom scope** based on what is disturbed behind finishes:
   - `opfrissen`;
   - `technisch vernieuwen`;
   - `herindelen`.
2. Explain consequences of changing layout, water and drainage routes.
3. Treat substrate + water management / waterproofing as a technical system, not as a tile choice.
4. Treat electrical work in a wet room as safety-sensitive; mention NEN 1010 at high level only.
5. Make ventilation a pre-close/pre-finish decision, with Milieu Centraal as source.
6. Include a current **VEH price level 2026** bathroom benchmark with room size, finish level and the explicit exclusion that demolition of the old bathroom is not included.
7. Explain that current market ranges cannot be merged blindly because scope differs.
8. Give a bathroom-specific quote comparison scope: demolition/disposal, substrate repair, water/drainage, electrical, ventilation, water-management/waterproofing, finishing, sanitary supply/installation, exclusions, unknowns, morework and handover.
9. Create a clear **dichtmaak-gate**: what must be decided/assigned before walls/floor are closed or tiled.

## SHOULD coverage

- planning dependencies rather than a universal duration;
- water/energy-saving opportunities from Milieu Centraal only after core technical scope;
- future-use/accessibility as a design question, without presenting one layout as universally required;
- regulatory escalation only when scope affects structure, regulated building work or another activity requiring a check.

## Information gain

### 1. Badkamerscope in 3 levels
Use what is disturbed behind the finish as the classification criterion, not how luxurious the sanitary ware is.

### 2. Techniekkaart achter de tegel
One compact map of the interfaces that must agree before finishes:
- indeling + maatvoering;
- water + afvoer;
- ondergrond + watermanagement/waterdichting;
- elektra + verwarming;
- ventilatie;
- doorvoeren/aansluitingen + responsibility.

### 3. Dichtmaak-gate
Before tiling/closing, the owner should know who owns each hidden layer/system and what has been checked. This is not a DIY procedure.

### 4. Scoped 2026 benchmark
Use VEH 2026 figures exactly with scope:
- 2 x 3 m: simple €4,095 / average €7,875 / luxury €19,925;
- 3 x 3 m: simple €4,650 / average €9,400 / luxury €22,400;
- incl. VAT and installation;
- old-bathroom demolition excluded;
- `average` = completely new bathroom by professional, non-premium tiles, no A-brand sanitary ware.

Do not convert this to one universal €/m² figure.

## Required evidence

- **Vereniging Eigen Huis — Wat kost verbouwen**, price level 2026: bathroom benchmark and scope/exclusions.
- **Milieu Centraal — Nieuwe badkamer**, current 2026 page: ventilation, moisture prevention, shower-WTW / water-saving / insulation opportunities.
- **NEN — NEN 1010 overview**: applies to new installations and modifications/extensions; no detailed bathroom-zone numbers unless the actual normative text is directly verified.
- **Consumentenbond**, updated April 2026: prepare before showroom and insist on a specified offer for comparison.
- **Rijksoverheid / Omgevingsloket** only for regulatory escalation, not as a claim that ordinary bathroom replacement always requires a permit.

## Data gaps / safety constraints

- No universal project duration.
- No detailed NEN wet-room distances, IP ratings or zone tables.
- No universal waterproofing product/system recipe.
- No certainty about hidden moisture cause without inspection.
- Electrical changes in the wet room: keep advice at planning/scope level and escalate appropriate design/execution.
- Potential asbestos or structural intervention: stop DIY framing and escalate.

## Proposed structure

1. Direct answer: start by defining how deep the renovation goes, not by choosing tiles.
2. `Welke badkamer-renovatie heb je eigenlijk?` — 3 scope levels.
3. `Wat moet achter de tegel al vaststaan?` — techniekkaart.
4. `De dichtmaak-gate` — what must be resolved before finishing.
5. `Wat kost een badkamer in 2026?` — scoped VEH benchmark + what is outside it.
6. `Waar ontstaan de grootste prijsverschillen?` — visible product vs hidden/building scope.
7. `Wat moet in een badkamer-offerte staan?` — bathroom-specific matrix.
8. `Planning: welke afhankelijkheden bepalen de doorlooptijd?` — no universal weeks.
9. Next decisions / links.

The final structure may shift during copy if a clearer reading flow emerges. Do not clone `keuken-renovatie` section-for-section.
