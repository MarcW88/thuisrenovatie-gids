## Role

Own the lifecycle, not the physical work order. Seven useful phases: inventory; design/scope; feasibility/rules; budget/offers; execution preparation; execution/change management; handover.

## Content rule

Each phase should answer what must be sufficiently clear before moving on. Do not turn the seven phases into the site's generic article template.

## Boundary

`renovatie-volgorde` answers which construction work comes first. This page answers when the homeowner researches, decides, contracts, executes and closes the project.
