## Evidence register

Primary current source: Vereniging Eigen Huis, `Wat kost verbouwen?`, price level 2026.

Useful benchmarks from that source:
- kitchen incl. installation: EUR 3,990-30,600 depending on level;
- bathroom 2x3m: EUR 4,095-19,925, excluding demolition;
- casco masonry extension 10m2: EUR 21,750-27,550, excluding opening, finishing, installations and interior;
- attic: EUR 6,565-19,925 depending on scope.

Broad whole-house ranges from commercial market guides may be used only as secondary orientation, clearly labelled indicative.

## Boundary

This page answers what work costs. `/renovatie-budget/` answers how a homeowner allocates and controls their money.

## Editorial rule

Do not hide behind "it depends". Give numbers when scope and source are clear, then explain the conditions behind them.
