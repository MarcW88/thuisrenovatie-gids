## 4. Price evidence selected

### Primary benchmark: Vereniging Eigen Huis, price level 2026
Bathroom prices are stated as average prices incl. VAT and installation and **exclude demolition of the old bathroom**.

- 2 x 3 m, simple: €4,095
- 2 x 3 m, average: €7,875
- 2 x 3 m, luxury: €19,925
- 3 x 3 m, simple: €4,650
- 3 x 3 m, average: €9,400
- 3 x 3 m, luxury: €22,400

VEH defines `average` as a completely new bathroom installed by a professional with non-premium tiles and no A-brand sanitary ware. Their drivers include tile choice, share/type of plastering, quantity/brand of sanitary ware and options such as floor heating.

### Secondary market context
Trustoo 2026 reports €8,500–€15,000 for a standard 5–10 m² complete renovation including basic sanitary ware and tiles, based on requests/offers on its platform. Homedeal publishes lower/broader ranges. Because scope and methodology differ, these sources should not be merged into one fake market average.

### Editorial consequence
Use VEH as the page's benchmark because its scope and exclusions are explicit. Mention that demolition, hidden repair, relocation of services and premium materials can move a project outside the benchmark. Do not create a single €/m² promise.

## 5. Information gain to build

### A. Three-level `badkamerscope`
1. **Opfrissen** — finishes/sanitary are changed while layout and hidden systems remain largely untouched.
2. **Technisch vernieuwen** — layout can remain, but substrate, water management, plumbing, electrical or ventilation is opened/rebuilt.
3. **Herindelen** — sanitary positions move and several hidden systems must be redesigned together.

The decision is based on what is disturbed behind the finish, not on the price or number of new products.

### B. `Techniekkaart achter de tegel`
A compact interface map that must be resolved before final product selection:
- maatvoering + indeling;
- water + afvoer;
- ondergrond + watermanagement/waterdichting;
- elektra + verwarming;
- ventilatie;
- interfaces/doorvoeren/responsibility.

### C. `Dichtmaak-gate`
Before tiling/closing walls and floor, the owner should know who is responsible for substrate preparation, water-management/waterproofing system, penetrations/joints, plumbing/electrical rough-in and ventilation. The page must not provide a DIY waterproofing or electrical recipe.

### D. `Offerte-scopekaart`
A bathroom-specific comparison matrix covering demolition/disposal, substrate repair, water/drainage, electrical, ventilation, water-management/waterproofing, tiling/finishing, sanitary supply/installation, exclusions, unknowns, morework, planning and handover.

## 6. GEO / citation opportunities

Useful extractable facts/answers:
- A bathroom renovation should be scoped by what changes behind the finish, not only by visible sanitary ware.
- Milieu Centraal: good ventilation in the bathroom helps prevent moisture problems and the renovation is a logical moment to review ventilation and energy/water-saving measures.
- NEN: NEN 1010 applies to new electrical installations and to modifications/extensions of existing installations; avoid reproducing detailed wet-room zones without direct normative verification.
- VEH 2026 bathroom benchmark with explicit room size, finish level and demolition exclusion.
- Consumentenbond: insist on a specified quote so offers can be compared.
