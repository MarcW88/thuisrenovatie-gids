# Research coverage — Opstijgend vocht

- Checked: 2026-09-13
- Decision: `DEEP_REWRITE`

## Intent
User sees low-wall moisture and is often already exposed to treatment-first commercial pages. The page must keep `rising damp` as a testable hypothesis.

## Evidence
Vereniging Eigen Huis current moisture guidance describes optrekkend vocht as moisture entering via foundation/crawlspace/walls through capillary action, with low-wall signs, but lists leakage, condensation and penetrating rain as overlapping alternatives.

Sources:
- https://www.eigenhuis.nl/problemenwijzer/vochtplekken-muur-plafond
- https://www.eigenhuis.nl/huis-verbeteren/klussen/zorgen-voor-je-huis/vocht-schimmel-in-huis

## Information gain
`opstijgend-vocht bewijskaart`: fits / speaks against / unknown / investigate.

## Anti-pattern
No injection, membrane or plaster system recommendation before source confirmation. No single moisture-meter result as proof.
