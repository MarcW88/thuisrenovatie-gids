## Intent

## Current evidence
### KCAF — signals of settlement
KCAF lists cracks, binding windows/doors, skew floors/walls, unusually high water under the floor and height differences as possible signals. It also states cracks can have other causes.
Source: https://www.kcaf.nl/kennisbank/signalen-verzakking/

### KCAF — first steps
KCAF advises documenting suspected damage with photos and dates and provides a structured repair/investigation pathway. It also operates a national foundation desk and a standardized QuickScan route.
Sources:
- https://www.kcaf.nl/wat-doet-het-kcaf/stappenplan-funderingsherstel/
- https://www.kcaf.nl/

### Rijksoverheid — 28 August 2026
The current Nationale Aanpak Funderingen emphasizes easier access to foundation-risk information and further investigation where risk is high. Risk information supports triage but is not itself proof of damage.
Source: https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2026/08/28/steun-voor-eigenaren-bij-funderingsschade
