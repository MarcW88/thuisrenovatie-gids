## Intent
The broad intent is symptom-led: moisture, mould, cracks, settlement or deformation. The main editorial risk is premature diagnosis from a single visible sign.

## Current evidence
### Milieu Centraal — Schimmels in huis
- mould is associated with moisture and insufficient fresh air;
- recurring or widespread mould can point to an underlying building defect or ventilation problem;
- cause should be addressed rather than only surface staining.
Source: https://www.milieucentraal.nl/huis-en-tuin/schoonmaken/schimmels-in-huis/

### Rijksoverheid — ventileren
- continuous ventilation is needed to remove moisture and pollutants;
- short airing is not equivalent to continuous ventilation;
- better-insulated homes make adequate ventilation more important.
Source: https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/vraag-en-antwoord/energie-thuis/hoe-kan-ik-mijn-huis-ventileren

### Vereniging Eigen Huis — moisture patterns
- moisture marks can originate from leakage, condensation, penetrating rain or rising moisture;
- low-wall patterns can be compatible with rising moisture but require differentiation from alternatives;
- monitoring whether a patch grows or changes is useful evidence.
Sources:
- https://www.eigenhuis.nl/problemenwijzer/vochtplekken-muur-plafond
- https://www.eigenhuis.nl/huis-verbeteren/klussen/zorgen-voor-je-huis/vocht-schimmel-in-huis

### KCAF — foundation signals
- cracks, binding doors/windows, skew floors/walls and height differences can be signs of settlement;
- these signs can have other causes and should be assessed in combination;
- KCAF recommends documenting damage and dates and provides a national foundation-problem desk / QuickScan route.
Sources:
- https://www.kcaf.nl/kennisbank/signalen-verzakking/
- https://www.kcaf.nl/wat-doet-het-kcaf/stappenplan-funderingsherstel/

### Rijksoverheid — Nationale Aanpak Funderingen
Current 28 August 2026 update confirms improving risk information and further investigation where risk is high.
Source: https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2026/08/28/steun-voor-eigenaren-bij-funderingsschade

## Information gain
The hub adds a reusable `probleemlogboek` and an evidence ladder:
1. observation;
2. correlation;
3. hypothesis;
4. research question;
5. treatment.
