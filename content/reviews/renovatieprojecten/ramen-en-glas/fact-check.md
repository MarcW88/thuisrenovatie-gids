## Sources verified

### Milieu Centraal — Kozijnen en deuren vervangen
Checked current page, last modified 8 July 2026.

Supported claims used in the page:

- first assess whether replacement is actually necessary; repair/adaptation may sometimes be possible;
- when frames/windows become more airtight, ventilation must be considered at the same time;
- double draught seals / good closing reduce unwanted air leakage;
- frame-to-wall joints and other seams must be properly sealed;
- water/rain detailing around windows/doors matters;
- when replacing frames at an uninsulated cavity, nature-protection rules under the Omgevingswet may be relevant because animals such as bats can use gaps near the frame;
- in frames/windows from before 1994, asbestos-containing putty or sealant may have been used.

Claim boundary preserved:

- the page says these issues **may** apply and routes concrete nature checks to the Omgevingsloket;
- it does not state that every pre-1994 frame contains asbestos;
- it gives no asbestos-removal instructions.

### Vereniging Eigen Huis — Kozijnen vervangen
Supported claims used:

- existing frames are not always suitable for thicker/heavier insulating glass;
- frame-to-wall sealing is an important installation detail;
- replacing windows/frames is a logical moment to review ventilation;
- guarantee terms should be visible and multiple quotations should be compared.

VEH's cost/subsidy example currently shown is dated February 2025. It is **not** presented as a 2026 price benchmark on this page.

## Cost claim review

Current 2026 commercial SERP sources use incompatible pricing units and scopes:

- per window/opening;
- per m² glass;
- per m² complete frame;
- whole-house totals;
- with or without glass, installation, demolition, finishing, access/steiger and ventilation.

Because no sufficiently consistent independent authoritative national 2026 benchmark was found, the page intentionally publishes **no universal national average**. It explains how to normalise the unit and scope before comparing prices.
