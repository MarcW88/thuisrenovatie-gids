# Fact check — Renovatieprojecten hub

- Route: `/renovatieprojecten/`
- Review date: 2026-09-13
- Status: PASS

## Claims checked

### Different project types have different technical dependencies
**PASS.** Milieu Centraal's current `Verbouwen en verduurzamen` guidance treats projects such as aanbouw and replacing kozijnen/ramen as distinct interventions with different links to insulation, ventilation, heating and the building envelope. The hub therefore does not claim one universal technical workflow.

### Define current situation / desired outcome before requesting quotes
**PASS.** Vereniging Eigen Huis `Verbouwen: maak een plan` advises clarifying the current situation and wishes before moving into quote requests. The hub translates that into project scope and project brief language without presenting it as a legal rule.

### Renovation/building projects may require project-specific regulatory checks
**PASS.** Rijksoverheid `Stappenplan bij bouwen en verbouwen` confirms that omgevingsplan, welstand, building rules and permit requirements may need to be checked. The hub only states that such checks can influence a project; it does not give project-specific legal verdicts.

### Foundation route is diagnosis-first
**PASS as editorial/safety boundary.** The hub does not diagnose foundation damage from symptoms and routes symptom/diagnosis intent to `/problemen-oplossen/funderingsproblemen/` before repair comparison.

### Windows/glass route vs energy glazing route
**PASS as IA boundary.** No factual conflict: the project route owns replacement scope/frames/installation/ventilation interfaces, while `/verduurzamen/dubbel-glas/` owns the HR++ vs triple energy decision.

## Excluded claims

- No national average project duration.
- No hub-level price ranges.
- No universal permit yes/no claims.
- No claim that a specific symptom proves foundation failure.

## Result

PASS — no central unsupported or time-sensitive claim blocks publication review.
