# Fact check — Funderingsproblemen

- Checked: 2026-09-13
- Result: **PASS**

Verified against current KCAF and Rijksoverheid information:
- cracks, binding doors/windows, skew floors/walls and settlement/height differences can be relevant signals but do not individually prove foundation damage;
- documenting damage with dates/photos is consistent with KCAF's first-step guidance;
- risk/context can justify further investigation but risk is not the same as confirmed damage;
- the current Nationale Aanpak Funderingen focuses on risk information, research, support and repair pathways;
- repair method selection is correctly deferred until sufficient investigation.

Safety boundary: no remote structural safety approval and no repair prescription from crack shape alone.

**PASS**.
