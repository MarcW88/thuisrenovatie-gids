# Fact check — Vochtproblemen

- Checked: 2026-09-13
- Result: **PASS**

Verified against current Milieu Centraal, Rijksoverheid and Vereniging Eigen Huis guidance:
- condensation, leakage, penetrating rain, cold details and low-wall moisture can create overlapping visible symptoms;
- continuous ventilation is functionally different from short airing;
- recurring/widespread mould should trigger cause investigation rather than only surface cleaning;
- low-wall moisture is not by itself proof of rising damp;
- one moisture-meter reading is not presented as an end diagnosis.

Safety boundary: no destructive inspection, injection treatment, hidden pipe work or construction modification is prescribed as DIY diagnosis.

**PASS**.
