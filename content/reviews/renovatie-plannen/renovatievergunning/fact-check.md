# Fact check — Renovatievergunning

- Route: `/renovatie-plannen/renovatievergunning/`
- Checked: 2026-09-13
- Result: `PASS`

## Claims checked

### Omgevingsloket result and duration

**Claim:** the `Woning verbouwen` Vergunningscheck uses the location and selected work, takes about 15–30 minutes, and can show whether the user must apply for a permit, submit a notification or provide information.

**Status:** PASS.

**Source:** Omgevingsloket — `Woning verbouwen`.

### Two rule layers

**Claim:** building/renovation can involve both an omgevingsplan activity and a technical building activity; a project therefore cannot safely be reduced to one generic `permit yes/no` rule.

**Status:** PASS.

**Sources:** IPLO technical-building-activity guidance; IPLO homeowner guidance for aanbouw/uitbouw/bijgebouw; Vereniging Eigen Huis used only as secondary homeowner-language confirmation.

### Multiple authorities

**Claim:** depending on location and activity, municipality, province, water board or national government rules can be relevant.

**Status:** PASS.

**Source:** Omgevingsloket — `Woning verbouwen`.

### Permit-free is not rule-free

**Claim:** even where a building activity is permit-free, Bbl rules and burenrecht can still apply and other permits may still be relevant.

**Status:** PASS.

**Source:** Rijksoverheid — `Vergunningvrij bouwen en verbouwen`.

### Application documents

**Claim:** a building/renovation permit application can require items such as drawings and structural calculations, and the Omgevingsloket indicates required submissions.

**Status:** PASS.

**Source:** Rijksoverheid — `Vergunning aanvragen voor (ver)bouwen`.

### Regular-procedure timing

**Claim:** for the regular omgevingsvergunning procedure, the decision period is normally 8 weeks and the competent authority may extend it once by up to 6 weeks.

**Status:** PASS.

**Source:** IPLO — `Procedure bij een aanvraag om een omgevingsvergunning waarvoor de reguliere procedure geldt`.

**Caveat applied in page:** the text explicitly states that other procedures, additional information or specific activities can change the total timeline, so 8 weeks is not presented as a guaranteed project duration.

### Dakkapel / ordinary maintenance examples

**Claim:** ordinary maintenance and some rear-facing dakkapellen can be permit-free under conditions.

**Status:** PASS as illustrative examples only.

**Sources:** Rijksoverheid — `Vergunningvrij bouwen en verbouwen`; IPLO — `Vergunningvrije dakkapellen`.

The page does not convert these examples into a universal checklist.

## Legal/editorial boundary

No statement on the page gives an individual homeowner a definitive permit conclusion. The official location- and activity-specific Vergunningscheck remains the authoritative practical next step.
