# Fact-check — rendement renovatie

- Route: `/renovatie-plannen/rendement-renovatie/`
- Checked: 2026-09-13
- Status: `PASS`

## Claims checked

### NVM / brainbay fixer-upper analysis

Claim used: in 2025, brainbay modelled an average value increase of about **€178,000** for a defined fixer-upper cohort improved to excellent condition, keeping living area constant.

Source: NVM / brainbay, `Kluswoning na verbouwing bijna 50% meer waard`, published 2026-07-23.

Verification: PASS.

Important qualification preserved in copy:
- specific fixer-upper cohort;
- model value, not universal realised profit;
- renovation cost, financing, purchase position, property type, size and location still matter;
- extra living area was excluded from the equal-area model.

### General housing-market baseline

Claim used: Brainbay reported Dutch home values **+3.7% YoY** in Q2 2026, with large local variation.

Source: Brainbay, `Woningen stijgen flink in waarde, vooral buiten de Randstad`, published 2026-07-22.

Verification: PASS.

Use: only to show why total before/after appreciation must not automatically be credited to renovation.

### Energy-label value context

Claim used: at the start of 2026, Brainbay reported that a comparable label-A home carried a modelled value premium of **more than 17%** versus label G.

Source: Brainbay, `Waardestijging koopwoningen zwakt af, verschil tussen energielabels groot`, published 2026-01-28.

Verification: PASS.

Qualification preserved: this is a market/model relationship and not a guaranteed return from one specific energy intervention.

### Milieu Centraal long-term energy prices

Claim used: for savings calculations, Milieu Centraal uses average long-term prices for 2026–2040 of **€1.37/m³ gas** and **€0.21/kWh electricity**.

Source: Milieu Centraal, `Besparingen: met welke energieprijzen rekenen we?`, current page checked 2026-09-13.

Verification: PASS.

### Extra usable floor area / kitchen and bathroom

Claim used: Vereniging Eigen Huis states that extra usable space such as an extension or usable attic often has the clearest relation to value, while kitchen/bathroom improvements are more comfort- and taste-sensitive.

Source: Vereniging Eigen Huis, `Wat kost verbouwen`, price level 2026.

Verification: PASS.

## Claims deliberately excluded

- universal ROI percentages for kitchen, bathroom, dakkapel, extension or full renovation;
- a claim that €178,000 is average renovation profit;
- a claim that a label improvement guarantees a fixed house-value percentage;
- invented probabilities or euro amounts for avoided future damage;
- a monetary value assigned to personal comfort.

## Conclusion

All central factual claims are supported and their scope/limitations are visible in the copy. No unresolved central claim remains.
