## Verified claims

- RVO lists five homeowner insulation categories: cavity wall, facade, glass, floor/ground and roof or attic-floor insulation.
- ISDE also covers qualifying heat pumps, solar boilers, ventilation, warm-network connection and, in applicable situations, electric cooking provision.
- 2026 ventilation support is €400 one time and requires combination with one or more qualifying insulation measures.
- Combining isolation with ventilation alone does not trigger the isolation-subsidy doubling rule.
- The isolation amount can double under the current combination logic when combined with another qualifying insulation measure or qualifying heat pump, solar boiler or warm-network connection within the relevant 24-month framework.
- Live RVO product/material registers contain meldcodes and current product-level subsidy information.
- RVO states for at least heat-pump and ventilation registers that an unlisted product may be submitted with technical documentation, but eligibility is not guaranteed and the application can be rejected.
- RVO application guidance requires preparation of evidence such as photos and measure/product details; timing must be checked per concrete measure.
