# Fact-check — Renovatiebudget

- Result: PASS

## Claims checked

### NN practical project buffer

Claim: Nationale-Nederlanden advises an extra 10–20% buffer for unexpected renovation costs.

Status: VERIFIED.

Source: Nationale-Nederlanden, `Wat kost een verbouwing?`, last updated 1 July 2026. The article advises clarifying the intended works, requesting several offers and allowing an extra 10–20% above the renovation budget for unexpected costs.

Editorial treatment: presented as consumer guidance, not as a universal engineering rule.

### Project contingency vs personal financial buffer

Claim: homeowners should not treat the renovation contingency as their only financial safety margin.

Status: VERIFIED WITH CAREFUL WORDING.

Source: Nationale-Nederlanden, `Je verbouwing betalen?`, last updated 23 June 2026. It advises reserving 10–20% of the renovation budget for unexpected renovation costs and also retaining a financial buffer after the renovation for other life events.

Editorial treatment: the page calls these two different pots and does not prescribe a personal buffer amount.

### Decide what falls away if offers exceed budget

Claim: homeowners should decide what can be removed or changed if offers exceed the available budget.

Status: VERIFIED.

Source: Vereniging Eigen Huis, `Verbouwen: maak een plan`. The planning guidance asks what the total budget is and what falls away if the offer is higher than the budget; it also distinguishes structural changes from aesthetic improvements.

Editorial treatment: converted into an editorial priority ladder (`MUST / SHOULD / COULD`). The labels themselves are our method, not a VEH framework.

### Stelpost meaning

Claim: a `stelpost` is an estimated amount whose exact cost is not yet known at contract stage.

Status: VERIFIED.

Source: Vereniging Eigen Huis, `Verbouwen: offerte en contract aannemer`. VEH explains that a stelpost is an estimated amount for a part of the renovation for which the final cost becomes clear during execution.

Editorial treatment: the recommendation to keep stelposten under `verwacht` rather than `vastgelegd` is our budget-control method, not a legal classification.

### Financing capacity and budget ceiling

Claim: financing possibilities and renovation budget should be treated as separate decisions.

Status: VERIFIED AS EDITORIAL DISTINCTION.

Source: Vereniging Eigen Huis separately covers the project budget and financing routes such as savings, mortgage increase and personal loans. The page does not claim a legal rule that the amounts must differ; it advises homeowners to set a conscious project ceiling rather than automatically equating available financing with desired spend.
