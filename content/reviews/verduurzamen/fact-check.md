# Fact check — `/verduurzamen/`

- Checked: 2026-09-13
- Workflow version: 2
- Result: **PASS**

## No fixed universal sustainability order
CONFIRMED.

Milieu Centraal's current `Stappenplan voor een energiezuinig huis` explicitly states that its order is not fixed and that homeowners can enter at a logical renovation moment.

Source: https://www.milieucentraal.nl/energie-besparen/energiezuinig-wonen/stappenplan-voor-een-energiezuinig-huis/

## Insulation / airtightness and ventilation are linked
CONFIRMED.

Current Milieu Centraal guidance explains that ventilation remains necessary as a home becomes better insulated and less dependent on uncontrolled air leaks.

Sources:
- https://www.milieucentraal.nl/energie-besparen/ventilatie/woning-ventileren/
- https://www.milieucentraal.nl/energie-besparen/isoleren-en-besparen/naden-en-kieren-dicht/

## Heating choice depends on house readiness and heat emission
CONFIRMED.

Milieu Centraal's heat-pump guidance uses insulation and suitability of the heat-emission system as key decision inputs.

Sources:
- https://www.milieucentraal.nl/tests-en-tools/keuzehulp-warmtepomp/
- https://www.milieucentraal.nl/energie-besparen/duurzaam-verwarmen-en-koelen/hybride-warmtepomp/

## Boiler replacement can be a logical entry moment
CONFIRMED.

The current Milieu Centraal steps and heating test both treat an ageing/replacement boiler as a valid trigger to accelerate the heating-system decision instead of waiting for a fictional universal sequence.

Source: https://www.milieucentraal.nl/energie-besparen/energiezuinig-wonen/verwarmingstest-is-jouw-huis-klaar-voor-een-warmtepomp/

## Solar planning should include future electricity demand
CONFIRMED.

Current Milieu Centraal solar guidance tells homeowners to account for future changes such as an all-electric heat pump or EV charging. The dedicated v2 PV page now owns the full sizing decision.

Source: https://www.milieucentraal.nl/energie-besparen/zonnepanelen/kosten-en-opbrengst-zonnepanelen/

## Summer comfort / external shading
CONFIRMED.

Milieu Centraal includes external shading together with insulation and ventilation in the basis of an energy-efficient home.

## Claims deliberately not made
- no universal cost ranking;
- no universal savings ranking;
- no fixed payback periods;
- no current subsidy amounts on the hub;
- no blanket rule that every house must complete all insulation before any installation decision;
- no equipment sizing on the hub.

## Final
**PASS** — central factual claims are supported and appropriately bounded.
