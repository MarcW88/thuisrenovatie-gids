## Sources verified

### Milieu Centraal — Dakisolatie
Checked current page on 2026-09-13.

Supported claims used in the page:

- first check whether roof insulation already exists and how thick / where it is;
- an unheated attic that can be closed off can be separated from the heated home by insulating the attic/loft floor;
- if the attic will be used as living/working/sleeping space, roof-plane insulation is the more logical route;
- pitched roofs can be insulated from inside; the actual existing build-up must be understood first;
- insulating a pitched roof from outside is especially logical when tiles/roof are already being renewed;
- outside insulation can raise the roof and requires renewed detailing at gutter and neighbouring roof junctions;
- flat-roof insulation should be treated as professional work and is normally installed from outside;
- Milieu Centraal advises against insulating a flat roof from the inside because of moisture, timber-rot and mould risk;
- mediocre existing insulation can be improved by adding a layer or replacing the old layer;
- the position of vapour-retarding layers matters because timber enclosed between two such layers can become too wet;
- current advice level for good roof insulation is Rd 3.8;
- protected animals and Omgevingswet duties can be relevant; if a bat or bird is encountered during work, work should stop and an ecologist should be involved.

Claim boundaries preserved:

- the page does not say every pitched roof can safely be insulated from inside;
- the page does not give a universal vapour-control recipe without knowing the construction;
- the page does not infer presence or absence of protected species remotely;
- the page does not turn flat-roof insulation into DIY instructions.

## Cost claim review

The article uses one source-bounded example from Milieu Centraal: approximately **€6,500 before subsidy for professionally insulating the pitched roof of an average corner house**.

This is clearly framed as:

- an orientation example;
- not a universal project price;
- dependent on roof area, access, existing layers, finishing and combined roof work.

Current 2026 commercial SERP sources publish materially different ranges for:

- internal pitched-roof insulation;
- external pitched-roof insulation;
- flat-roof insulation;
- attic-floor insulation;
- roof renovation including insulation.

The page therefore does not publish one universal `€/m²` price.
