# Fact check — Vloerisolatie

- Result: `PASS`

## Sources verified

### Milieu Centraal — Vloerisolatie

Current page checked 13 September 2026.

Supported claims used:

- insulation fixed directly to the underside of the floor is the easiest/effective route when the crawlspace is accessible and workable;
- around 35 cm clear height below the floor beams is presented as a rule of thumb, while contractors can apply different access requirements;
- low/no crawlspace has alternative routes including insulation from above, a new floor or ground insulation;
- crawlspace ventilation openings should remain open;
- the crawlspace hatch, gaps and service penetrations are relevant execution details;
- Milieu Centraal recommends at least `Rd 3.5 m²K/W` for good floor insulation and `Rd 5 m²K/W` with underfloor heating;
- existing moderate insulation can sometimes be improved, but the existing layer/build-up matters.

Boundary preserved:

- the page does not state that 35 cm guarantees installability;
- it does not prescribe one material for all floors;
- it does not claim that every wet crawlspace prevents insulation.

### Milieu Centraal — Bodemisolatie

Current page checked 13 September 2026.

Supported claims used:

- ground insulation is especially relevant when floor insulation is not practically possible;
- floor insulation generally has a stronger direct thermal/comfort effect because ground insulation leaves a cold/ventilated air layer between floor and insulation;
- a low crawlspace around 20–35 cm is relevant context for considering ground insulation;
- floor condition, services and crawlspace ventilation require checking first;
- ordinary ground insulation is not automatically sufficient for underfloor heating; very good insulation at/in the floor is preferred where feasible.

Boundary preserved:

- the page does not claim ground insulation has no value;
- it does not promise that ground insulation solves an unidentified leak or groundwater problem.

### Milieu Centraal — Checklist offertes vloerisolatie

Current page checked 13 September 2026.

Supported claims used:

- crawlspace accessibility and height belong in quote preparation;
- for a wooden floor, moisture condition should be assessed before insulation;
- material and achieved Rd should be explicit;
- crawlspace ground covering, ventilation, hatch and service penetrations should be discussed in the quote.

### Vereniging Eigen Huis — floor/crawlspace guidance

Current pages checked September 2026.

## Cost claim review

Milieu Centraal currently publishes example costs by house type and a scoped floor-vs-ground example. The page deliberately does not convert these into a universal national €/m² figure.

The article states that price comparisons require the same:

- route;
- actual insulated area;
- access/workability;
- moisture preparation;
- existing insulation handling;
- repair/slope/finish scope.

This is supported by the variation between the identified project routes.
