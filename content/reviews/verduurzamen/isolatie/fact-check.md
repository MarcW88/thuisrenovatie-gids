# Fact check — Isolatie

- Route: `/verduurzamen/isolatie/`
- Checked: 2026-09-13
- Result: `PASS`

## Sources verified

### Milieu Centraal — Stappenplan voor een energiezuinig huis
Checked current page on 2026-09-13.

Supported claims used in the page:

- there is no single fixed order for making every home energy-efficient;
- existing insulation, ventilation and solar shading should first be checked;
- drawings, invoices, previous-owner information and physical checks can help establish the current insulation state;
- a logical starting point is parts of the heated home that are not yet insulated;
- insulation improvements can be combined with renovation moments that already open or replace a building element.

Claim boundary preserved:

- the article does **not** turn this into a universal `roof first` or `cavity first` sequence;
- it does not treat building year as proof of current insulation.

### Milieu Centraal — Verbeterjehuis / Verbetercheck
Current page last modified 24 June 2026.

Supported use:

- dwelling characteristics and current insulation are inputs for a house-specific improvement plan;
- the tool is appropriate as a route for dwelling-specific cost/saving orientation rather than copying one universal house total.

### Milieu Centraal — Alles over isoleren
Checked current page on 2026-09-13.

Supported claims:

- the main envelope routes include roof, walls, floor and glazing;
- costs and savings depend on dwelling/start situation;
- improving from no insulation to good insulation is materially different from improving an already moderately insulated house;
- combining insulation with other renovation work can be a logical moment.

No specific universal savings percentage or payback period is reproduced on our page.

### RVO — ISDE 2026 / meldcodelijst isolatiematerialen
Current meldcode list checked 10 September 2026.

Supported claims:

- ISDE has qualifying insulation routes including roof/zolder-vlieringvloer, facade, cavity wall, floor/bottom and glazing;
- subsidy depends on measure/product and m²;
- official meldcodes and qualifying product details are maintained by RVO;
- the subsidy amount for insulation measures can double when more than one qualifying insulation measure is carried out, including certain combinations with other ISDE measures, when the current 24-month and other conditions are met.

Claim boundary preserved:

- the page says `kan verdubbelen`, not that every combination automatically doubles;
- no frozen subsidy amount is presented as universally current;
- users are told to check current RVO conditions before ordering/applying.

### Volkshuisvesting Nederland — Natuurvriendelijk Isoleren
Checked current page on 2026-09-13.

Supported claims:

- protected species can affect cavity-wall insulation;
- current routes can involve eDNA, a municipal species-management plan or provincial permit/research requirements;
- the applicable route depends on location and situation.

Claim boundary preserved:

- no universal permit verdict;
- no statement that every cavity contains protected animals;
- no DIY instructions for excluding/removing animals.

### Vereniging Eigen Huis — Isolatie woningen per bouwjaar
Checked current page on 2026-09-13.

Supported boundary:

- building year is a useful starting clue;
- prior residents may already have improved insulation.

The page therefore treats build year as context, never proof.

## Cost claim review

The page intentionally does not publish one `cost to insulate a house` total because scope varies by:

- current state of each building element;
- dwelling type and surface area;
- technical method;
- access/scaffolding;
- removal and preparation;
- finishing;
- subsidy eligibility.

Milieu Centraal's current examples and Verbetercheck are used as orientation routes instead of flattening those differences into one unsupported total.

## Safety / regulatory review

- moisture/leaks/wood or structural uncertainty are stop conditions, not remotely diagnosed;
- no instruction to insulate over a moisture problem;
- nature protection is framed as a pre-work check with current official routing;
- no universal permit or protected-species conclusion;
- ventilation is treated as a planning dependency, not as an optional afterthought;
- no claim that a heat pump always requires full insulation first.

## Conclusion

`PASS`

The central decision claims are supported by current independent/official sources and the page keeps time-sensitive subsidy and nature-protection details bounded to current official checks.
