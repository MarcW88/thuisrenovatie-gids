## Sources verified

### Milieu Centraal — Triple, HR++ of vacuümglas

Current page checked; last modified 18 August 2026.

Supported claims used in the page:

- U-value is the relevant thermal-performance metric for glazing; lower is better;
- ordinary older double glazing in regularly heated rooms can be worthwhile replacing with HR++ or triple;
- in unheated rooms, replacing ordinary double glazing is less compelling unless frames are being renewed anyway;
- when existing frames are still good and technically suitable, HR++ is the main recommended glass-only route;
- existing-frame suitability depends on thickness/weight and the construction of fixed/opening parts;
- triple glazing is primarily advised in combination with new insulating frames in the current Milieu Centraal route;
- vacuum glazing can achieve very good insulation with a relatively thin build-up but is more expensive and context-dependent;
- ventilation must be considered when glazing/airtightness improves;
- external shading is important for summer comfort at sun-exposed glazing;
- external condensation can occur on well-insulating glazing and is not automatically a defect.

Claim boundaries preserved:

- the page does **not** state that triple technically requires new frames in every possible case;
- it does **not** state that every existing frame can accept HR++;
- vacuum glazing is presented as a contextual option, not a universal premium winner;
- no fixed energy saving or payback claim is published without a specific scenario.

## Cost claim review

The page intentionally avoids a universal national €/m² or whole-house price.

Price comparison is normalized for:

- glass type + declared U-value;
- glass-only versus glass + frame replacement;
- removal/disposal;
- rebate/glazing-bead adjustments;
- opening-part/hardware adjustments;
- ventilation provisions;
- installation/sealant/finishing;
- subsidy before/after framing.

Milieu Centraal's current cost examples remain source-specific scenarios and are not generalized into a national average.
