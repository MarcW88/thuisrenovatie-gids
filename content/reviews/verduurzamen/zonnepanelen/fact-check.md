# Fact check — Zonnepanelen

- Route: `/verduurzamen/zonnepanelen/`
- Checked: 2026-09-13
- Result: **PASS**

## Verified claims

### Salderingsregeling
PASS.
- Rijksoverheid states the salderingsregeling ends on **1 January 2027**.
- From that date exported electricity can no longer be offset against imported electricity.
- A feed-in compensation remains; supplier contract conditions and permitted feed-in costs affect household economics.

Source: Rijksoverheid, `Salderingsregeling stopt in 2027`, checked 2026-09-13.

### Direct self-consumption
PASS.
- Milieu Centraal states direct self-consumption becomes more important after saldering ends.
- Published household self-consumption percentages are averages, not guarantees; the page therefore does not promise a fixed percentage for the reader.

### Future electricity demand
PASS.
- Heat pumps and EV charging can materially change electricity demand and timing.
- The page uses future demand as a sizing input without pretending to calculate a final system size remotely.

### Roof / orientation / shade
PASS.
- Current Milieu Centraal guidance treats roof suitability and shade as pre-installation factors.
- Page avoids a universal south-vs-east/west winner or a fixed kWh/Wp yield claim.

### Meterkast and connection
PASS.
- Page requires project-specific electrical assessment but explicitly avoids claiming every PV installation requires 3-phase or a heavier grid connection.

### Registration
PASS.
- Current consumer guidance advises notifying/registering the installation with the grid operator before/around installation. Page keeps responsibility in the execution checklist and does not build technical sizing around an administrative shortcut.

### Home battery
PASS.
- Milieu Centraal confirms a battery can raise self-consumption but also has significant cost/capacity/environmental considerations.
- Page correctly treats it as a separate decision rather than a default upsell.

### VAT
PASS.
- Belastingdienst currently applies 0% VAT to delivery and installation of PV on or near a dwelling, subject to scope/conditions.
- Page correctly warns that adjacent works such as roof strengthening, complete distribution-board replacement and home batteries may fall outside that 0% scope.

### Costs / ROI
PASS.
- No universal payback period, savings percentage or €/panel figure is presented as fact.
- Current Milieu Centraal examples are acknowledged only as scoped examples; page centres scope normalisation.

## Safety / boundaries
PASS.
- no DIY electrical design;
- no remote structural approval;
- no fixed cable/protection prescription;
- no financial guarantee;
- no universal grid-connection upgrade claim.

## Final
**PASS** — no blocking factual or freshness issue found.
