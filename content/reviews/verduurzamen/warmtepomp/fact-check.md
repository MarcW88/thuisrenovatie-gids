# Fact check — Warmtepomp

- Route: `/verduurzamen/warmtepomp/`
- Reviewed: 2026-09-13
- Result: **PASS**

## Claims checked

### All-electric suitability
PASS.

Milieu Centraal states that a fully electric heat pump is suitable when the home is at least reasonably to well insulated and the heat emitters can deliver enough heat at a lower water temperature.

### 50 °C heating test
PASS.

Milieu Centraal uses a practical test with the boiler water set to about 50 °C. The page correctly presents this as a strong practical readiness signal, not as a substitute for project-specific heat-loss/dimensioning work.

### Hybrid versus fully electric
PASS.

The page does not claim a universal winner. Hybrid remains a possible route for homes that are not yet fully ready for all-electric operation; fully electric requires broader readiness including domestic hot water and space.

### Radiators / emitters
PASS.

The page correctly avoids the false shortcut that existing radiators are automatically suitable. Capacity at the selected lower design temperature must be assessed.

### Dimensioning
PASS.

The page explicitly rejects copying boiler nameplate capacity as heat-pump sizing. It asks for a documented sizing basis and design conditions.

### Domestic hot water
PASS.

For all-electric systems, domestic hot water and storage volume/space are correctly included as a separate design decision.

### Outdoor unit noise
PASS.

Milieu Centraal currently states the applicable boundary context as maximum 45 dB at the property boundary during daytime and 40 dB in evening/night. The page correctly avoids inventing a universal distance rule and requires project-specific placement assessment.

### Electrical connection
PASS.

The page correctly frames the electrical connection and consumer unit as items to assess, not as an automatic instruction to upgrade the grid connection.

### Municipal heat plan
PASS.

Milieu Centraal advises checking municipal plans before committing to all-electric where a district heating route may be planned.

### 2026 hybrid obligation
PASS.

Rijksoverheid confirms that the proposed 2026 heating-system standard was dropped. The page correctly says there is no general national obligation to install at least a hybrid heat pump on boiler replacement on the basis of that old proposal.

### ISDE
PASS.

RVO's heat-pump meldcodelijst was checked 10 September 2026. Meldcodes and subsidy amounts are appliance-specific; the page therefore avoids a single generic subsidy amount.

## Claim boundaries

- no universal cost or payback claim;
- no remote heat-loss calculation;
- no guaranteed savings;
- no fixed outdoor-unit distance rule;
- no blanket statement that every old or new house is suitable;
- no superseded 2026 obligation claim;
- subsidy is not used as technical decision driver.
