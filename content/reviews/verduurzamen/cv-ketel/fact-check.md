# Fact check — Cv-ketel

- Route: `/verduurzamen/cv-ketel/`
- Checked: 2026-09-13
- Workflow version: 2
- Result: **PASS**

## Claim review

### 1. No general hybrid obligation from 2026
**PASS.** Rijksoverheid states that the cabinet abandoned the planned heating-installation efficiency standard for 2026.

Source: https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/documenten/2024/10/31/rapporten-onderzoeken-voor-vervallen-normering-verwarmingsinstallaties-in-2026

### 2. Replacement moment is a logical point to compare alternatives
**PASS.** Milieu Centraal explicitly frames cv-boiler replacement as a moment to consider hybrid or fully electric alternatives.

Source: https://www.milieucentraal.nl/energie-besparen/verwarmen-met-gas/cv-ketel-vervangen/

### 3. Age alone is not presented as a hard technical replacement threshold
**PASS.** The page intentionally avoids a universal age cutoff. Commercial and consumer sources often use average lifetimes, but that is not converted into a mandatory replacement rule.

### 4. Older open/semi-open boilers require extra safety attention
**PASS.** Milieu Centraal explains that old open installations use indoor combustion air and can pose greater combustion-gas risk when air supply is insufficient.

Source: https://www.milieucentraal.nl/energie-besparen/verwarmen-met-gas/cv-ketel-onderhoud-en-instelling/

### 5. Gas-combustion work requires a CO-vrij certified company
**PASS.** Rijksoverheid states that installation, maintenance and repair of cv boilers and other gas-combustion appliances must be performed by certified companies and that DIY work on these appliances is not allowed.

Source: https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/vraag-en-antwoord/gezond-en-veilig-wonen/hoe-voorkom-ik-koolmonoxidevergiftiging-in-mijn-huis

### 6. Shared flue / combustion-air systems require explicit apartment/VvE attention
**PASS.** Rijksoverheid states that in apartment buildings a shared flue-gas discharge / combustion-air system may be involved and that the VvE is responsible for maintenance/control of the shared system.

Source: same Rijksoverheid CO page.

### 7. Flue and combustion-air scope is part of meaningful boiler replacement
**PASS.** Supported by the official safety context above. The page does not prescribe a technical method; it requires the scope to be assessed and priced.

### 8. HR, hybrid and all-electric are different system scopes
**PASS.** The page avoids treating them as interchangeable quotations. This is consistent with Milieu Centraal's separate system routes and current commercial SERP structure.

### 9. Hot-water comfort is separated from space heating
**PASS.** This is a design/scope distinction, not a fixed sizing claim. The page does not prescribe a CW class or kW value.

### 10. Heat-pump readiness is handed off instead of duplicated
**PASS.** The page routes to `/verduurzamen/warmtepomp/` for 50 °C test, emitters, sizing, noise and electrical readiness.

### 11. Electric cv / induction cv is not normalized as equivalent sustainable alternative
**PASS.** Milieu Centraal discourages electric cv / induction-cv because of high electricity use.

Source: https://www.milieucentraal.nl/energie-besparen/verwarmen-met-gas/cv-ketel-vervangen/

### 12. ISDE boundary is attached to qualifying heat-pump product/meldcode
**PASS.** RVO's current heat-pump meldcodelijst provides approved products, meldcodes and subsidy amounts and was checked by RVO on 10 September 2026.

Source: https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/isde/meldcodelijsten/warmtepompen

### 13. No universal 2026 boiler replacement price is asserted
**PASS.** Commercial SERP prices mix device-only, standard installation, flue changes, thermostats, VAT treatment and hybrid scope. The page therefore uses scope normalization instead of one national average.

### 14. Quote matrix includes commissioning/inregeling and exclusions
**PASS.** This is a procurement-quality requirement, not a universal technical prescription.

## Safety boundary

PASS.

- no DIY gas instructions;
- no remote safety diagnosis;
- no instruction to modify flue-gas or combustion-air components;
- certified execution requirement explicit;
- shared-flue responsibilities surfaced.

## Regulation / freshness

PASS.

The page explicitly distinguishes the abandoned 2026 proposal from current policy and uses current September 2026 RVO product data rather than a stale generic subsidy amount.

## Cost-data gate

PASS.

No universal boiler price, lifetime, savings rate or payback period is published as fact.
